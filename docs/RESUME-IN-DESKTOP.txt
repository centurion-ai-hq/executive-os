WHY IT IS SPLIT THIS WAY ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

She types nine words into the terminal. Everything heavy happens in the app,
where she can see it and where pasting a block is easy.

The terminal line does not finish the setup and does not write the "setup
complete" marker, so the app picks up mid-interview rather than thinking it is
already done and skipping the last questions.

else, it will not find her answers and will start over. That is why you take the
path down the phone rather than trusting her to find it later.
